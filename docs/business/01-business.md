# 1. The business
Muayana (معاينة) is a pocket tool for **one audit engineer** who inspects buildings under construction
or at handover. On site he photographs every defect he sees: a broken brick, a cracked wall, a dirty
surface, a loose socket. Later he sorts each photo under a **trade category** (نقاشة، كهرباء، سباكة،
محارة…), assigns it to a **worker** of that trade, sends it to the worker (usually on WhatsApp), and
follows it until it is solved.

- The app only makes the engineer's own work easier. Nobody else signs in. **DECIDED**
- **Money:** none. The app holds no prices, costs or payments. **DECIDED**
- Language: Arabic first (RTL), English available. Category names exist in both. **GUESS**
