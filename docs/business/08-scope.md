# 8. Scope
**Built in v1:** sign-in, sites, categories, workers, capture with an offline queue, sort inbox,
assign, WhatsApp send, statuses and history, issues list with filters, worker pages, dashboard, CSV
exports, Arabic/English, light/dark, installable app.

**Planned:** none yet.

**Left out on purpose:** worker accounts or a worker portal; more than one engineer; PDF reports; links
for owners or clients; automatic WhatsApp sending (Business API); pinning on floor plans; a native
mobile app; prices or costs.
